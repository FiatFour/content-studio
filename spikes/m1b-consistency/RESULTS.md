# M1b consistency spike: results so far

Status: **arms C (baseline) and A (IP-Adapter) measured; arm B (per-character LoRA) not started. No decision yet (D8).**
Plan: `PLAN.md`. Harness: `harness.py`, fixture `fixture.json`. Spike commit base `3cc7e6a`; ComfyUI `cdf272f`, `ComfyUI_IPAdapter_plus` `a0f451a5` (GPL-3.0, last commit April 2025), weights `h94/IP-Adapter` (Apache-2.0).
Images and contact sheets are generated media and stay outside git in `~/ai/m1b-out/` (`c2/contact_C.png`, `a04/contact_A.png`, `a/contact_A.png`).

## Setup

RTX 5060 Laptop 8 GB, WSL2 with 11.5 GiB RAM, DreamShaper XL Turbo v2.1, 8 steps, dpmpp_sde karras, cfg 2, 768×1344 (D4). Two original characters (a grandmother and an uncle), six scenes each with fixed seeds, same prompt text and seeds in every arm. `make dev` and Ollama were stopped, ComfyUI restarted before each arm. Reference images were generated by the same checkpoint (one front view per character, seed base 7000); the owner has not yet picked them, so this is a provisional reference.

## Measurements

Time is wall clock from `POST /prompt` until history reports success; cold is the first image of an arm (model load), warm is the median of the other 11. VRAM is whole-GPU `memory.used` sampled at 1 Hz.

| Arm | Cold | Warm median (min-max) | Peak VRAM | Valid? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C baseline, rerun | 35.9 s | **10.4 s** (10.3-11.6) | 6974 MiB | yes |
| A IP-Adapter, weight 0.4 | 43.2 s | **12.4 s** (11.4-16.7) | 7072 MiB | yes |
| C baseline, first run | 48.1 s | 65.0 s (27.4-117.3) | 7006 MiB | **no, slowed** |
| A IP-Adapter, weight 0.7 | 55.5 s | 38.2 s (26.8-150.7) | 7072 MiB | **no, slowed** |

The two slowed runs ran 3 to 10 times slower in the middle of the arm, and the host clock shows a gap of about 38 minutes during arm A at 0.7, so the laptop probably stalled or slept (known risk, `docs/assessment.md`). SM clocks of 180 to 500 MHz were logged during those runs. Their timings are kept only as a warning: **timing on this machine is not reliable unless clocks and sleep are controlled**. The VRAM peaks are stable across all four runs (about 7.0 GB, so one GPU job at a time still applies, and the LLM and TTS cannot be loaded at the same time).

IP-Adapter adds about 2 s per image (about 20 %) and about 100 MiB at weight 0.4. This is one clean sample per arm, not a statistically solid number.

## Observations (advisory; the owner judges quality)

- **Baseline (C).** Scenes follow the prompts, but the character is not stable: the grandmother's blouse changes between yellow and green and the sarong pattern changes; the uncle's face, hair and even rendering style change from shot to shot (some shots look like a photograph, some like a cartoon). A text-only description is not enough for continuity.
- **IP-Adapter at weight 0.7.** Outfit and face stay very stable, but the reference pose, framing and studio background are copied into every shot, so the scene prompts (kitchen, market, hammock) are ignored. Unusable for storytelling.
- **IP-Adapter at weight 0.4.** Outfit and identity stay stable and the scenes appear (sofa, kitchen, market, lake). A bias towards the reference pose (hands on hips, front view) remains, and the uncle's face still varies a little (one shot has a grey beard). The reference was a full-body front view, which probably drives the pose bias.
- All arms render closer to photographs than to an adult-animated sitcom. That comes from the D4 checkpoint and the shared style prompt, not from the technique, and matters for the style decision regardless of D8.
